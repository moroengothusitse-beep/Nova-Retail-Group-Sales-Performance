# Nova-Retail-Group-Sales-Performance
Nova Retail Group — Sales Performance

<img width="809" height="165" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0f71ef36-5ba0-4b13-8aaf-e047755655f4" />


A total revenue was reached of R8.51M from 705 orders, generating a total profit of R2.97M. This indicate a strong overall profit performance, with profit representing approximately 35% of revenue.

The orders are fairly balanced across sales channels, with Online contributing 448 orders (63.55%) and Store contributing 257 orders (36.45%). Online is clearly the dominant sales channel.

Revenue shows recurring peaks throughout the period, with the highest monthly revenue exceeding R450K at the end of 2024. This suggests strong seasonal demand and increased sales momentum toward year-end.

North is the top-performing region, generating R4.98M in revenue from 731 orders, ranking 1st among all regions.
East generated R4.17M in revenue and 600 orders, making it the second-largest contributor to overall sales.
West is the lowest-performing region, generating R3.83M in revenue from 574 orders, despite only trailing South by a small margin.

North is the top-performing region, generating R4.98M in revenue from 731 orders.
Online sales account for 448 orders (63.55% of total orders), making Online the dominant sales channel.
Robert Rodriguez is the highest-value customer, spending R207,942.65, followed by Ella Lopez at R179,696.30. A small group of customers contributes significantly to overall revenue.

Recommendations:

An increase in the investment on the Online channel, which generated 63.55% of all orders, to drive further sales growth.

We need to focus marketing and sales efforts on the West region, which records the lowest revenue (R3.83M) and order volume (574 orders), to improve regional performance.

An Implementation on loyalty programs and targeted promotions for high-value customers, such as Robert Rodriguez (R207,942.65 spend), to encourage repeat purchases and retention.
